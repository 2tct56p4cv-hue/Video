# CAPCO Technology Team — Animated Corporate Video
### Production Script & Voice-Over Document
**Format:** 16:9 widescreen · ~4:20 runtime · English VO · 2D animated corporate style
**Audience:** Internal Capco presentation
**Palette:** Navy blue / blue / white / teal, Capco corporate identity
**Music:** Subtle upbeat corporate underscore, mixed under narration throughout, swelling slightly at the final scene

---

## Narrator Direction
Professional, confident, friendly. Moderate pace (~145–155 words/minute). Warm on the human
moments (Scene 1, Scene 4's bug gag, closing scene), crisper and more declarative on the
capability scenes (2–7) so on-screen labels land in sync with the VO beats noted below.

---

## SCENE 1 — ONE TECHNOLOGY TEAM
**Timecode:** 0:00 – 0:25 (25s)

**Visual:** Open in a modern financial-services office. Cut between four specialists working
in isolation — a data analyst studying a dashboard, a developer coding, a QA tester running a
suite, a cloud engineer at an infrastructure console. Camera moves fluidly between them
(dolly/pan, not hard cuts). As the VO reaches "That is where our Technology Team comes in,"
thin glowing digital lines extend from each screen and begin connecting the four workspaces
into one visible network.

**VO:**
> "Behind every successful technology transformation, there is much more than technology.
> There are people who understand the data, build the solutions, test their quality,
> modernize the platforms, and make sure everything works when it matters most.
>
> That is where our Technology Team comes in."

**On-screen text (appears as the lines connect, ~0:20):** `PEOPLE + TECHNOLOGY + BETTER OUTCOMES`

**Transition out:** One of the connecting data-lines rushes toward camera and dissolves into
streaming data particles — becoming the opening of Scene 2.

---

## SCENE 2 — DATA & ANALYTICS
**Timecode:** 0:25 – 0:55 (30s)

**Visual:** We're inside a digital data environment. Streams labeled *Banking Data*,
*Customer Data*, *Regulatory Data*, *External Data* pour in from multiple directions,
tangled and fragmented. A data engineer gestures and the streams begin to comb into orderly
parallel pipelines. Small floating labels/icons pass through the pipeline in sequence:
`Python` → `SQL` → `BigQuery` → `GCP` → `Airflow` → `dbt`. The pipeline empties into a
dashboard that builds itself live — bar chart rising, pie chart filling, trend line drawing.

**VO:**
> "Our Data Engineers and Analytics specialists turn complex information into something
> businesses can actually use.
>
> We build reliable data pipelines, improve data quality, and transform large volumes of
> financial data into trusted insights.
>
> From SQL and Python to BigQuery, GCP and Airflow, we help move data from complexity to
> business value."

**On-screen text (builds progressively under the dashboard, ~0:48):**
`CLEAN → TRANSFORM → ANALYZE → INSIGHT`

**Transition out:** One glowing data point breaks off the finished dashboard, arcs across the
screen, and lands as a blinking cursor — becoming the first character of a line of code that
opens Scene 3.

---

## SCENE 3 — SOFTWARE ENGINEERING & CLOUD
**Timecode:** 0:55 – 1:25 (30s)

**Visual:** The cursor from Scene 2 finishes typing a line of code; the camera pulls back to
reveal a developer's workstation, code compiling in real time. Behind the developer, a
diagram of a legacy monolith block visibly fractures and reassembles into a cluster of
connected microservices. Cloud provider marks (AWS / Azure / GCP) glow in sequence above a
rotating globe/cloud icon as language icons (`Java`, `Python`, `Apache Camel`) drift past the
screen.

**VO:**
> "Our engineers build the technology behind those insights.
>
> We develop applications, integrations, and cloud-native solutions designed for modern
> financial institutions.
>
> Whether we are working with Java, Python, microservices, or cloud platforms such as AWS,
> Azure, and Google Cloud, the objective is the same: solutions that are scalable, secure,
> and resilient."

**On-screen text (~1:18):** `BUILD. CONNECT. SCALE.`

**Transition out:** A green "build successful" checkmark pulses on the code editor and flies
forward, morphing into a QA dashboard checklist icon that opens Scene 4.

---

## SCENE 4 — TESTING & QUALITY
**Timecode:** 1:25 – 1:55 (30s)

**Visual:** A QA engineer's screen shows automated test cases firing rapidly — checkmarks
ticking down a list (`Test Planning`, `Test Execution`, `Defect Management`, `Quality
Reporting`). Split-screen dashboards show a pass-rate ring climbing toward 98% and a defect
counter ticking down. Tool marks (`Tosca`, `NeoLoad`, `LoadRunner`) appear briefly beside the
relevant test types (functional, performance, load).

**Humor beat (silent sight-gag, ~1:42–1:48):** A small red cartoon "bug" icon creeps along a
pipeline toward a "Production" door. An automated test scanner sweeps over it, flags it in
red, and the bug does a sheepish about-face and scurries off. No dialogue — let the visual
land, then cut back to the dashboard.

**VO:**
> "But building something is only part of the journey.
>
> Our Testing and Quality specialists make sure solutions actually work — reliably, securely,
> and at scale.
>
> From test strategy and automation to performance testing and QA leadership, we identify
> problems before they become business problems."

**On-screen text (~1:52, appears with a green quality badge):** `HIGHER QUALITY. GREATER CONFIDENCE.`

**Transition out:** The green pass-rate ring completes into a full circle and spins into an
infinity-loop shape — becoming the DevOps pipeline icon that opens Scene 5.

## SCENE 5 — DEVOPS & PLATFORM MODERNIZATION
**Timecode:** 1:55 – 2:25 (30s)

**Visual:** A developer pushes a commit; a glowing package icon travels automatically through
an animated infinity-loop pipeline: `BUILD → TEST → DEPLOY → MONITOR`, each stage lighting up
as the package passes through. Small container and Helm-chart icons stack and snap into
place; an ArgoCD-style sync arrow spins. Behind the pipeline, a cluttered rack of legacy
servers visually dissolves and re-forms into a clean, minimal cloud-native platform diagram.

**VO:**
> "Our DevOps and platform specialists connect development with delivery.
>
> We automate pipelines, implement GitOps processes, and help organizations modernize legacy
> technology.
>
> The result is faster delivery, more resilient platforms, and less manual complexity."

**On-screen text (~2:20):** `AUTOMATE → MODERNIZE → ACCELERATE`

**Transition out:** The now-modern cloud platform diagram's glow extends outward into a city
skyline of a digital bank — becoming the establishing shot of Scene 6.

## SCENE 6 — RISK, COMPLIANCE & DATA GOVERNANCE
**Timecode:** 2:25 – 2:55 (30s)

**Visual:** Inside a digital bank environment, streams of transaction data flow across the
screen as clean, glowing lines. A governance specialist reviews a monitoring dashboard;
most transactions flow through calmly, but one line flashes amber, is isolated, inspected,
and cleared or escalated — while the rest continue undisturbed. Concept icons appear beside
the flow: `AML`, `Transaction Monitoring`, `Data Governance`, `Data Quality`,
`Regulatory Reporting`, `Audit`.

**VO:**
> "In financial services, however, technology also needs trust.
>
> Our specialists combine technology expertise with knowledge of AML, transaction
> monitoring, data governance, data quality, and regulatory requirements.
>
> We help ensure that the right data is available, the right controls are in place, and
> transformation remains compliant."

**On-screen text (~2:50, appears beside a shield icon):** `RIGHT DATA. RIGHT CONTROLS. GREATER TRUST.`

**Transition out:** The shield icon settles onto a bridge structure that begins extending
across the screen — becoming the migration bridge of Scene 7.

## SCENE 7 — MIGRATION & DELIVERY
**Timecode:** 2:55 – 3:25 (30s)

**Visual:** Split composition: an aging on-prem banking system (server racks, dated UI) on
the left; a bright modern cloud environment on the right. A glowing bridge forms between
them. Small character and data-block icons move carefully across the bridge in coordinated
waves as four milestone markers light up in sequence beneath it: `PLAN → MIGRATE → VALIDATE
→ GO LIVE`. Specialists on both sides gesture and coordinate, checking items off a shared
tracker.

**VO:**
> "And when organizations need to move from legacy technology to something new, our
> migration and delivery expertise helps make that change possible.
>
> We coordinate complex migrations, cutovers, and transformation activities so that
> technology, data, and people arrive at the destination together — with minimal disruption
> to the business."

**On-screen text (~3:20):** `FROM LEGACY TO MODERN.`

**Transition out:** Camera pulls up and back from the bridge, revealing it as one thread in a
much larger web of connections — opening into the wide view of Scene 8.

## SCENE 8 — THE CAPABILITIES CONNECT
**Timecode:** 3:25 – 3:55 (30s)

**Visual:** Wide shot reassembling every specialist and environment from Scenes 2–7 into one
connected diagram. As each capability is named in the VO, its icon pulses and a bright line
draws to the next one, building a growing network in real time: Data Engineering → Analytics
→ Cloud/DevOps → Testing → Governance → Migration, all converging toward the center of frame.

**VO:**
> "The real strength of the team is not any single technology or role.
>
> It is how these capabilities connect.
>
> Data Engineering works with Analytics. Developers work with Cloud and DevOps. Testing
> protects delivery. Governance creates trust. And migration expertise helps bring everything
> safely into the real world."

**On-screen text (~3:52, appears at the center of the network):** `CONNECTED EXPERTISE`

**Transition out:** The fully connected network contracts smoothly into a single point of
light at center frame, which then expands outward into the final scene's skyline.

## FINAL SCENE — ONE TEAM
**Timecode:** 3:55 – 4:20 (25s)

**Visual:** Camera pulls back to reveal the full cast of specialists standing together,
looking out over a modern financial-district skyline threaded with glowing digital
infrastructure. Seven labels rise from the group and drift upward — `DATA`, `ENGINEERING`,
`CLOUD`, `QUALITY`, `DEVOPS`, `GOVERNANCE`, `MIGRATION` — converging into one word at the
top of frame: `IMPACT`. Hold, then settle into the elegant Capco closing card.

**VO:**
> "We may come from different technical backgrounds, but we share one objective: using
> technology to solve real problems and create better outcomes for our clients.
>
> Different skills. Connected expertise. One Technology Team."

**Closing card (holds several seconds, on-screen text):**
```
ONE TEAM.
CONNECTED DELIVERY.
LASTING IMPACT.

CAPCO
Ideas. Technology. Lasting Impact.
```

## Global Animation Direction (applies to every scene)
- No static holds while narration plays — every shot carries continuous, purposeful motion:
  characters interacting with screens, data flowing, charts building, code typing, clouds
  connecting, tests firing, infrastructure morphing, dashboards updating, migration objects
  moving, people collaborating.
- Every scene transition is a **match-cut / morph**, not a hard cut or slide: a visual element
  from the outgoing scene physically becomes the seed of the next (data point → code cursor;
  build checkmark → QA checklist; pass-rate ring → CI/CD loop; platform glow → skyline; shield
  → bridge; bridge pull-back → capability network; network → final skyline). This is what
  makes the 4-minute film read as one continuous story instead of eight slides.
- Keep on-screen text minimal — one short line/tagline per scene, timed to appear right after
  its cue in the VO above, never crowding the frame. Let narration carry the explanation.
- Diverse, international cast throughout; modern offices, dashboards, cloud/banking visual
  motifs; clean vector character style — professional, not exaggerated or slapstick. The one
  deliberate humor beat is the Scene 4 bug gag; keep every other scene warm but business-toned.
- End on the closing card held for several seconds — clean enough to remain on screen during
  applause or a live transition to the next agenda item.

## Production Notes / Handoff
- **Primary reference:** attached Capco storyboard (8-panel layout) — see `/storyboard/` for
  the shot-by-shot breakdown built from it.
- **Recommended pipeline:** this script is written to be handed directly to a 2D
  motion-design studio, or to an AI video/animation generation tool (e.g. a
  storyboard-to-video model) as scene-by-scene prompts — each scene section above is a
  self-contained prompt block (visual + VO + on-screen text + duration).
- **VO recording:** record scene-by-scene against the timecodes above; total spoken content
  runs close to the ~4:20 target at a moderate pace — trim pauses rather than words if a
  tighter 4:00 cut is required.
- **Deliverables to request from a vendor/tool:** MP4, 16:9, 1920×1080 minimum, mixed VO +
  music stems delivered separately for future re-cuts/localization.
